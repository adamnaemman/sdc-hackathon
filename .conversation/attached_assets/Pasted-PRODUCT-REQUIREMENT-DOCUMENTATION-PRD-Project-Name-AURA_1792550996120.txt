PRODUCT REQUIREMENT DOCUMENTATION (PRD)Project Name: AURA-Workforce (Adaptive Upskilling & Role Alignment)Track: Domain 1 - The Aiversecity Future-Ready Workforce DomainTarget UN SDGs: SDG 4 (Quality Education: 4.3, 4.4), SDG 8 (Decent Work: 8.2, 8.5), SDG 9 (Innovation: 9.5), SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities: 10.3)1. Problem Statement & ScopeModern employees and job seekers face career vulnerability due to rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence. Current upskilling initiatives rely on static lists that do not adapt to real-time labor market shifts.AURA-Workforce provides an AI-driven, continuous-learning advisory system that:Translates live job market and technology signals into directional skill forecasts.Evaluates an individual's existing profile against future-ready roles.Generates personalized, step-by-step reskilling roadmaps linked to specific UN Sustainable Development Goals.Accounts for market uncertainty through multi-scenario disruption modeling.Out of Scope (As per competition rules):In-app proprietary video course hosting/content creation.Commercial payment gateway processing.Direct corporate hiring applicant tracking system (ATS) replacements.2. User PersonasPersona A: Sarah - Vulnerable Administrative ExecutiveBackground: 32 years old, 7 years experience in corporate procurement and manual data management.Pain Point: Worried that ERP automation and generative AI tools will make her position obsolete in 18 months. Does not know which AI skills are legitimate vs. passing fads.Goal: Wants an actionable, low-cost learning pathway that builds on her organizational strengths toward higher-value roles (e.g., AI Operations Specialist or Sustainable Supply Chain Analyst).Persona B: Daniel - TVET / University Career CounselorBackground: Coordinates graduation pathways for 1,200 diploma students annually.Pain Point: University syllabus reviews take 2-3 years, meaning students graduate with outdated toolsets.Goal: Needs dynamic labor trend data to advise students on relevant micro-credentials that match real industry demand.3. Functional SpecificationsF1: Skills Profile IntakeCaptures user background via CV upload (PDF/TXT) or a guided 4-step form (Role, Industry, Known Skills, Soft Competencies).The intake parser extracts and maps inputs into a standardized JSON taxonomy:{
  "profile_id": "usr_demo_01",
  "current_role": "Procurement Assistant",
  "years_experience": 5,
  "skills": ["Vendor Communication", "Manual PO Processing", "Microsoft Excel", "Inventory Auditing"],
  "preferences": {"target_sectors": ["Smart Logistics", "Tech"], "hours_per_week": 6}
}
F2: AI-Trend Signal LayerMaintains a queryable data layer of emerging and declining roles.Displays primary data source (e.g., "Kaggle Open Job Market Dataset", "arXiv AI Taxonomy"), refresh timestamp, and growth/decline rate ($\Delta\%$).F3: Future-Role Matching EngineMatches skill vectors against future role demand vectors via RunPod GPU vector inference.Outputs a quantitative Fit Score (0-100%) with an explicit breakdown of retained transferable skills and critical skills gaps.F4: Personalised Reskilling PathwayGenerates an ordered, modular DAG curriculum for missing skills with estimated completion time (weeks/hours) and direct links to accredited free/low-cost learning materials (Coursera Free Tier, edX, HRD Corp-aligned modules).F5: SDG Mapping & Contribution MetricsEvery recommended future role and micro-skill is tagged with corresponding UN Sustainable Development Goal targets (e.g., Targets 4.3, 4.4, 8.2, 8.5, 9.5, 13.3).F6: Disruption Scenario Modeling (Uncertainty Handling)Toggles between Moderate Disruption (linear tool adoption; 4-year transition runway) and Aggressive Disruption (rapid agentic deployment; 18-month transition runway). Match scores and transition urgency update dynamically.4. Non-Functional RequirementsExplainability & Transparency: All AI recommendations must cite underlying data signals.Performance: Role matching and pathway generation execute within $\le 2.5\text{ seconds}$ on the live Replit workspace with RunPod GPU acceleration.Deployment Hosting: Hosted on Replit Core web environment with zero server administration requirements.Data Privacy & PDPA Compliance: Zero storage of personally identifiable information (PII); synthetic profiles only.
